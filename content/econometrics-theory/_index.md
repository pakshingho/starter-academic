Econometrics Theory is currently an outline-first course structure. The aim is to turn it into a numerical tour of econometrics, where each chapter later combines theory with simulations, worked examples, and intuition-building computation.

## Course at a glance

- Format: handbook outline, to be expanded into a self-paced numerical course
- Scope: chapter and section titles only for now
- Direction: simulations, numerical experiments, and structural-model intuition
- Status: ready for later chapter expansion

2. [Asymptotic Theory](asymptotic-theory/)

9. [Hypothesis Testing](hypothesis-testing/)

11. [Reading List](reading-list/)

## Planned throughline

The source note moves from mathematical and asymptotic foundations into simultaneous-equation models, identification, estimation, testing, and boundary cases. In the expanded version, each of those blocks will become a numerical chapter with simulation-based intuition rather than a transcription of lecture notes.
