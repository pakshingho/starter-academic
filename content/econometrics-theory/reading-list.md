This page collects the reading list currently associated with the course.

## Books

- H. White, *Asymptotic Theory for Econometricians*. Academic.
- R. Serfling, *Approximation Theorems for Mathematical Statistics*. Wiley.
- T. Amemiya, *Advanced Econometrics*. Blackwell.
- C. R. Rao, *Linear Statistical Inference and Its Applications*. Wiley.
- D. A. Harville, *Matrix Algebra for a Statistical Perspective*. Springer.
- P. Schmidt, *Econometrics*. Dekker.
- D. Sargan, *Lectures on Advanced Econometric Theory*. Blackwell.
- E. Malinvaud, *Statistical Methods of Econometrics*. North-Holland.
- P. J. Dhrymes, *Topics in Advanced Econometrics, Vol. II*. Springer.

## Articles and Chapters

- Amemiya, T. (1975). *Journal of Econometrics*.
- Amemiya, T. (1977). *Econometrica*.
- Chamberlain, G. (1986). *Journal of Econometrics*.
- Chamberlain, G. (1992). *Econometrica*.
- Hansen (1982). *Econometrica*.
- Hatanaka, M. (1976). "Several efficient two-step estimators for the dynamic simultaneous equations model with autoregressive disturbances." *Journal of Econometrics*, 4, 189-204.
- Hausman, J. (1975). "An instrumental variable approach to full information estimators for linear and certain nonlinear econometric models." *Econometrica*, 43, 727-737.
- Hausman, J. (1982). "Specification and estimation of simultaneous equations model." In *Handbook of Econometrics*, Vol. 1, edited by Z. Griliches and M. D. Intriligator. North-Holland, 392-448.
- Newey, W. (1991). *Econometrica*, 59.
- Phillips, P. C. B. (1976). "The iterated minimum distance estimator and the quasi-maximum likelihood estimator." *Econometrica*, 44, 449-460.
- Phillips, P. C. B. (1982). "On the consistency of the FIML." *Econometrica*, 50, 1307-1324.
- Rothenberg, T. J. (1971). "Identification in parametric models." *Econometrica*, 39, 577-591.
- Robinson, P. M. (1988). "The stochastic difference between econometric statistics." *Econometrica*, 56, 531-548.
- Robinson, P. M. (1991). "Best nonlinear Three stage least squares estimation of certain econometric models." *Econometrica*, 59, 755-786.
